Brand Identity & Campaign Design

Wolves Café

The Brief: A new coffee shop in Port Said's crowded marina district needed to avoid being "just another café." The owner had the location and the beans — but no visual identity that would make people remember the name.

The Tension: Every competitor used brown tones and generic coffee icons. Blending in meant dying slowly. The brand needed to feel dangerous, memorable, and premium without alienating families.

The Turn: I developed a wolf+coffee cup mark with aggressive geometry and high-contrast black/gold palette. The wolf symbolized loyalty (pack mentality = community), while the crown detail signaled "king of the block."

The Resolution: The identity launched across 12 touchpoints — cups, signage, social campaigns, and staff uniforms. Within two months, the café became a recognizable landmark in the area, with customers specifically requesting "the wolf coffee place."

Deliverables: Logo system (primary + reversed + monochrome), social media campaign templates, promotional posters, menu design, branded cup mockups, storefront signage specs.

06
Packaging Design, Logo Design & Product Labeling

Cleaner — Dish Cleaner

The Challenge: Cleaning product manufacturer needed a complete packaging system for a new dish soap line that would stand out on crowded supermarket shelves, communicate efficacy instantly, and meet Egyptian regulatory requirements for chemical products.

My Role: Full brand creation — logo design, primary packaging label (front & back), product visualization, and promotional pricing integration.

The Solution: Created the "Dish Cleaner / كلينر" bilingual wordmark with a bold, trustworthy blue primary supported by energetic yellow and appetite-red accents. Front label leads with product benefit and appetite-triggering lime photography. Back label delivers full regulatory compliance: ingredients, warnings, usage directions, manufacturer data, barcode, and recycling symbols.

Results: Packaging approved for mass production; product launched with special introductory pricing. Label system designed for easy flavor/scent variant swapping while maintaining brand architecture.

About

Problem-first design for businesses that need results.

I'm a graphic designer based in Egypt with 2+ years of experience building visual systems for companies across 15+ industries. My work spans brand identity, packaging, social media campaigns, print design, apparel, and 3D visualization.

I don't start with aesthetics — I start with the business problem. Whether it's a coffee shop needing to differentiate in a saturated market, a pharmaceutical company promoting an event, or a cleaning product fighting for shelf attention, every design decision is tied to a measurable outcome.

I work in both Arabic and English, and I understand the cultural and regulatory nuances of designing for Middle Eastern markets — from Ramadan campaign sensitivity to product label compliance.

How I Work

From brief to deliverable in 4 steps

Discovery

30-minute call to understand your business problem, competitors, and constraints. I ask about shelf position, target demographic, and production specs — not just "what colors do you like."

Strategy

Color psychology, competitor audit, and 2-3 visual directions. You get mood boards with rationale — not random Pinterest dumps. Every choice connects to your business goal.

Design

2 rounds of revisions with clear feedback structure. No unlimited changes that drain budget — focused iterations that move toward the best solution fast.

Delivery

Production files, source files, and a style guide. Packaging includes print-ready PDFs with bleed. Digital assets come in all platform sizes. You're never stuck with one format.
